ArkGram is an independent, unofficial messaging client that uses the Telegram API. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Telegram.

Keep conversations together
Use your Telegram account for private chats, groups, channels and shared media. Switch between multiple accounts from one application.

Choose your tools
Configure translation, optional message filtering and interface preferences. Connect your own supported AI provider and model for translation and text tools. Requests to a custom provider use your own credentials and are subject to that provider's availability, pricing and terms.

Explore before signing in
The clearly labelled offline demo includes sample conversations, contacts and a local message composer. Demo messages are not sent to Telegram. Network messaging and calls require a real Telegram login. Demo settings use the real application settings.

Your choices matter
When you use an external translation or AI provider, selected content is sent to the provider you configure. Review its privacy terms before using it with personal information.

ArkGram is open source. Source code and project information: https://github.com/MoeclubM/Arkgram
