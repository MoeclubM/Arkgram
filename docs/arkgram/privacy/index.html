Privacy policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026 · Developer: MoeClub · Contact: moecaa@telecom.moe

This policy applies to ArkGram, including its Play, beta and standalone distributions. ArkGram is an independent, unofficial client using the Telegram API. MoeClub develops this client; Telegram operates the underlying messaging service. ArkGram is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Telegram.

Your choice of provider changes where data goes.

Telegram features use Telegram services. Optional external translation and AI features send the content needed for the selected operation to the provider endpoint configured in the app. A custom endpoint may be operated by a different organisation from the model vendor named in its API format.

2. Device permissions

Android permissions allow features such as contact synchronisation, selecting media, recording voice/video, calls, notifications and optional location sharing. You can grant or revoke permissions in Android settings. Denying a permission affects the feature that needs it; it does not create an offline substitute for that feature.

3. Local storage and credentials

The app stores account sessions, message/media caches, preferences, translation results, filter rules and configured API credentials on your device. Provider keys and prompts are stored in app preferences; this policy does not claim that they are encrypted by ArkGram. Android app storage protections apply. Removing a provider/key removes the app’s saved configuration, but does not revoke the key with its issuer. Revoke it with the provider if necessary.

Downloaded or exported files can be stored outside private app storage and may remain after sign-out or uninstall. Android or device backup/restore behaviour depends on the device and app configuration.

4. Optional services and transport

External translation and AI are optional. They operate when you request an action or enable an automatic/chat translation feature. You can stop using them, disable automatic translation, change the provider, or remove its credentials in settings. The configured endpoint and its protocol determine the destination and transport security; use HTTPS endpoints for sensitive data.

Providers may retain requests or use them under their own policies, account settings and agreements. ArkGram does not control their storage, model training, moderation or deletion. Review the actual endpoint operator’s privacy policy before submitting private content. A translation or AI request can create a provider-side copy even if its source chat uses end-to-end encryption.

5. Telegram and other recipients

Telegram’s privacy policy applies to its messaging service. Cloud chats and secret chats have different storage and security properties; do not assume that all chats or integrations are end-to-end encrypted. Bots, mini apps, opened websites, payment providers and map services may independently receive data when you use them.

The ArkGram enhancements do not add a default MoeClub relay for Telegram chats or custom AI requests. A provider or proxy you configure determines its own recipient. This statement does not cover information you intentionally send to support, this website, or a MoeClub endpoint you explicitly configure.

6. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local preferences, sessions and caches remain until removed through the relevant app controls, Android’s “Clear storage”, or uninstall, subject to device backup/restore. Exported files must be removed separately. Leaving a demo conversation clears its locally composed sample messages; the demo does not create a Telegram account. Demo settings are real app settings and persist until changed or cleared.

Telegram account, cloud-message and synced-contact retention/deletion are controlled through Telegram’s service and privacy settings. External translation/AI providers handle their own copies. Removing the app or clearing its cache does not delete Telegram cloud data or provider-held requests.

For steps to delete local data, a Telegram account, provider data or support correspondence, see ArkGram account and data deletion. You may contact moecaa@telecom.moe with privacy or support-data requests. Support correspondence is kept while needed to handle the request, related follow-up or applicable legal obligations; you can ask us to delete correspondence under our control.

7. Website, security and international processing

These policy pages contain no analytics scripts or advertising trackers. The hosting server may record request information such as IP address, user agent, time and requested path for operation and security. Contacting us by email sends your email address, message and attachments to our mail service.

Telegram, Google, your configured providers and their infrastructure may process data in other countries. No client, provider or network can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device protected and avoid sharing authentication codes, session files or API keys with support.

8. Age requirements and changes

ArkGram is not directed to children. You must meet the age and account requirements of Telegram, the services you use and applicable law. Material changes to ArkGram’s data handling will be reflected in an updated policy and, where required, an in-app notice or consent request. The effective date above identifies this version.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 18 日 · 开发者：MoeClub · 联系邮箱：moecaa@telecom.moe

本政策适用于 ArkGram 的 Play、测试版和独立分发版本。ArkGram 是使用 Telegram API 的独立非官方客户端，由 MoeClub 开发；底层通讯服务由 Telegram 运营，ArkGram 与 Telegram 没有隶属、背书或赞助关系。

提供商选择决定数据去向。

Telegram 功能使用 Telegram 服务；可选的外部翻译和 AI 功能会把所需内容发送至应用中配置的提供商端点。API 格式中的厂商名称不代表自定义端点一定由该厂商运营。

1. 功能涉及的数据

  • 登录与通讯：手机号、认证和会话信息、个人资料、自愿同步的联系人、消息、附件、群组和频道信息，以及技术连接信息，由客户端与 Telegram 用于认证、同步和消息投递。你主动交互的收件人、群组、频道、机器人或其他服务会接收相应内容。
  • 媒体、通话和位置：你选择的文件、录音录像输入、主动分享的位置及通话连接信息，用于对应操作。根据 Telegram 通话设置与连接方式，通话对方可能获知网络地址；地图提供商可能处理地图与位置请求。
  • 外部翻译：待翻译文本或字幕、源语言和目标语言、请求元数据会发送至所选服务，例如 Google、DeepL 或自定义模型端点。启用整段聊天或自动翻译后，该功能还可能处理后续消息。提供商同时接收处理请求所需的网络与认证信息。
  • 可选 AI：所选文字、摘要范围内的消息、文章正文、字幕、指令或风格提示词、模型及请求设置，会直接发送至你配置的提供商端点，并按需附带 API 密钥。将原本由 Telegram 提供的界面功能切换为自选模型，意味着输入改由该提供商接收。
  • 通知与平台服务：推送注册令牌、设备和应用技术信息、通知投递所需数据，可能由 Telegram、Google Firebase Cloud Messaging 及 Google Play 服务处理，具体取决于分发版本和设备环境。
  • 支持与诊断：你主动发送的支持信息和附件，以及你启用或分享的日志，用于处理问题。分享前请检查内容，因为日志和附件可能包含敏感信息。

2. 权限、本地存储与凭据

Android 权限用于联系人同步、媒体选择、录音录像、通话、通知及自愿位置分享等功能。你可在系统设置中授予或撤销权限；拒绝权限会影响依赖该权限的功能。

账号会话、消息和媒体缓存、偏好、翻译结果、过滤规则及配置的 API 凭据保存在设备上。提供商密钥和提示词使用应用偏好存储，本政策不声称 ArkGram 对这些配置另行加密；适用 Android 应用存储保护。删除本地密钥不会撤销提供商处的密钥，需要时请自行向提供商撤销。导出或下载到应用私有目录之外的文件可能在退出或卸载后仍保留，设备备份与恢复行为取决于设备和应用配置。

3. 外部服务、安全与数据共享

外部翻译和 AI 为可选功能，只在你发起操作或启用自动／聊天翻译时执行。你可停止使用、关闭自动翻译、更换提供商或删除凭据。目标端点及其协议决定数据接收方和传输安全性，处理敏感信息时请使用 HTTPS。

提供商可能依据自身政策、账号设置和协议保留或使用请求。ArkGram 不控制它们的留存、训练、内容审核或删除。即使原聊天使用端到端加密，调用翻译或 AI 也可能在提供商处产生额外副本。

Telegram 服务适用其隐私政策。云端聊天与私密聊天的存储和安全属性不同，不能假定所有聊天或扩展功能都使用端到端加密。机器人、小程序、支付服务、地图及你打开的网站可能独立处理相应数据。ArkGram 增强功能不会默认通过 MoeClub 中继转发聊天或自选 AI 请求；你配置的代理、服务端点，以及主动发送的支持信息和网站访问不在此说明范围内。

4. 留存与删除

本地偏好、会话和缓存保留至你使用应用相关控制、Android“清除存储空间”或卸载移除，并受设备备份恢复行为影响。导出文件需要单独删除。离开演示聊天后，其中新输入的样例消息清空；演示不会创建 Telegram 账号，但演示中的设置会真实保存，直至你修改或清除。

Telegram 云端数据与账号由 Telegram 的功能和政策控制；外部翻译或 AI 提供商分别处理其副本。卸载 ArkGram 或清除缓存不会删除 Telegram 云端数据或提供商保存的请求。具体步骤见账号与数据删除说明。

隐私或支持数据请求请联系 moecaa@telecom.moe。支持邮件会在处理问题、必要跟进及适用法律义务所需期间保留，你可请求删除由我们控制的支持通信。

5. 网站、跨境处理、年龄与更新

这些政策页面不含分析脚本或广告跟踪器。托管服务器可能为运行和安全记录 IP、用户代理、访问时间和路径等请求信息。发送支持邮件会将邮箱、正文和附件交由邮件服务处理。Telegram、Google、自选提供商及其基础设施可能在其他国家或地区处理数据，任何客户端和网络都无法保证绝对安全，请勿向支持人员发送验证码、会话文件或 API 密钥。

ArkGram 不面向儿童，你需符合 Telegram、所用服务及适用法律的年龄和账号要求。数据处理方式发生重大变化时，我们会更新本政策，并在需要时提供应用内提示或征求同意；页首日期标识当前版本。
